# MIT KEMAR normal-pinna HRTFs

Credit: Gardner, W. G., and Martin, K. D. (1995). “HRTF measurements of a KEMAR”, Journal of the Acoustical Society of America 97, 3907–3908.

Original measurements copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory. The authors provide the data free without restrictions on use provided they are cited in research or commercial applications. SOFA conversion by Piotr Majdak and Michael Mihocic, Acoustics Research Institute, OeAW.

Source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
SOFA distribution: https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/mit/mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa

`kemar.bin` is generated by `tools/prepare-hrtf.py` (run from the repository root). Preparation is a common bounded diffuse-field EQ and one global normalization, preserving interaural differences. Hashes and dimensions are in `kemar.json`. Runtime has no Python, HDF5, filesystem or network dependency.

Binary format: APHR magic, little-endian u32 measurement count / taps per ear / sample rate, then f32 unit directions (forward, left, up), then f32 kernels in measurement / ear / sample order. This is an internal embedded asset format, not a public graph serialization contract.
